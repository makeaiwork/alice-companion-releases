# Alice Companion 0.1.0

- First macOS release for Apple Silicon and Intel, requiring macOS 15 or later.
- Use Alice AI with your own Yandex account in a desktop window or a compact edge companion.
- Start voice conversations with configurable keyboard shortcuts and shared microphone permission.
- Translate, summarize, correct, improve, shorten, and expand text with built-in prompt tools.
- Recognize Alice Companion by its new Dock icon and monochrome menu-bar logo.
- Check for signed updates from the app menu or Settings, with automatic checks at launch and every six hours when enabled.

Alice Companion is an unofficial client and is not affiliated with Yandex. Chat and voice features depend on the availability of the Alice AI service.
